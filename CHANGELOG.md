# MS32 Companion App Changelog

## v1.0.1 — 16 October 2026

Quality-of-life and spontaneous-flight update.

- Expanded Spontaneous Flights with Airport-to-Airport, Helicopter / Local Exploration, and Aircraft Testing / Free Flight modes.
- Added local city and country Passport stamping for exploration and test flights.
- Added selectable Free Flight Radio and Solo Flight Radio channels for spontaneous flights.
- Added Open to Join and Solo Flight access options.
- Journey Points verification for spontaneous flights now follows the pilot's selected Radio channel.
- Active Community Flights now show whether a spontaneous flight is Open to Join or Solo.
- Removed the 15-minute Flying Now posting cooldown so pilots can quickly correct and repost a flight.
- Split Journey Points reporting into Overall, Community Flight, and Spontaneous Flight views while retaining one combined Journey Points total for rank progression.
- Added filtered leaderboards for Overall, Community Flights, and Spontaneous Flights.

## v1.0.0

Initial public release of the MS32 Companion App / Global Passport.
